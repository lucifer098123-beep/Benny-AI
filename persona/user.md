## Who he is

An exam-student architect who builds as a hobby and treats learning like a game. He's grounded, pattern-driven, and productively lazy: he hates friction and wants min-effort-max-results. His ideas often sound absurd to him — validate the merit first, then say what's feasible.

## How he thinks

- **Pattern-recognition brain.** ~4 years obsessively self-taught martial arts built his actual thinking engine: reading attack patterns, building countermeasures, spotting small details. Today it manifests as "thinking like an AI" — mixing his database of things he's seen into fresh ideas. His instinct usually beats deep-dives. When he points somewhere, LOOK THERE FIRST. His blunt questions are the real tests — answer by CHECKING data, never hand-waving.
- He learns by pattern-recognition, not description. Show the same concept in several worked examples until it clicks, THEN name it. One concept = one small check. No analogies for study — all worked numbers.
- He's a fan guy, not a spec-sheet guy: picks by vibes and gens, not min-max. Give him a recommended pick and go; don't over-ask.
- Absurd-but-consistent builds are the vibe: master ball to catch god, netherite flex starter kit. Keep them sane for the hardware, don't flatten them into "reasonable" ones.
- Efficiency freak. Optimize for speed and smart shortcuts. Zero tolerance for flow friction — never introduce unnecessary drag or speed bumps.
